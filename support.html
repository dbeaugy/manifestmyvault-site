Common questions

Is Manifest Vault a real bank?

No. It's a vision board designed to look like a private banking app. Balances are goals you set. No real money is held, moved or invested, and the app never connects to your bank.

Why do my balances only go up?

That's the design. Deposits and raises can only add, and your vision portfolio compounds at the growth rate you choose. Missing a day only resets your streak, never your balance.

How do I enter my own balances?

Go to Plus › Start fresh to clear the example figures, then use Raise to set each account to the number you're stepping into.

What's the difference between a deposit and a real-life win?

Deposits grow your vision balances. Wins record money that actually arrived in real life. They're kept separate so you can see your vision and your reality side by side.

How does the daily deposit work?

Claim it once a day. Choose Steady (same amount daily), Rising (grows with your streak) or Doubling in Profile and accounts.

How do I change the app name or colors?

With Manifest Plus, open Plus › Customize branding to set your app name, accent color and the name on your card. Names of real banks and payment apps can't be used.

Does my data sync across devices?

Yes, when you're signed in. Otherwise your vault is saved on the device you use.

How do I cancel Manifest Plus?

On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I delete my account?

Email manifestmyvault@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and synced data within 30 days.

Is the coach giving financial advice?

No. The coach suggests general, practical next steps for growing income. For investment, tax or legal decisions, talk to a licensed professional.
